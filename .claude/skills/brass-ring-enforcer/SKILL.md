# Brass Ring Enforcer

## Directives
1. Verify that the `KTHA` brass-ring mark is rendered as the final closing stroke on every digital page (via `KathaThread`).
2. Verify that in forms or flows, the `KTHA` stamp only appears when the flow is complete/valid (via `KNarrativeThread`).
3. Screen readers must narrate the mark as "Katha maker's mark — complete" via `aria-label` or `sr-only`.
