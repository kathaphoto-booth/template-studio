# The Wedge — LA/OC Competitor Receipts (2026-07-16)

**Question.** Does any LA/OC photo-booth rental company let a prospective client
see their own personalized print (names, date, palette) **live, before booking**?

**Finding.** Across 12 named, currently-operating competitors surveyed on
2026-07-16, **none does**. The market splits into three patterns, none of which
puts a personalized proof in front of the client before they commit:

| Pattern | Who | Receipt |
|---|---|---|
| (b) Template gallery only, team customizes after | Selfie Booth Co · Photo Booth SoCal (basic) · Feature Booth | "clients choose a template from our library first. Then our design team customizes it" (selfieboothco.com); "Select from our design templates" (photoboothsocal.co); "Select from our extensive library… or opt for a custom design crafted by our in-house team" (featurebooth.com) |
| (c) Design-by-email proof after booking | Captured Celebrations · Tipsy Twins · Insta Photo Booth · Stay Golden · Orange Box · Pixster · Steven K Media · Jenuine Photo Co | "We send a digital proof of your custom overlay for approval before the event, typically 5–7 days in advance… two rounds of revisions" (capturedcelebrations.com); "Our graphic design team will reach out 30 days before your event… we'll send you a questionnaire… multiple samples… unlimited edits" (tipsytwinsphotobooth.com); "Our designers will work with you directly… weeks before your event" (instaphotoboothrental.com); "sent me a preview of the template… the week before our wedding" (staygoldenphotobooth.com review); "4-8 weeks prior: Complete design, backdrop and features" (orangeboxphotobooth.com); contact-based post-booking (pixsterphotobooth.com); "custom print designs" bundled in package (stevenkmedia.com); "works with customers to design custom templates" (jenuinephotoco.com) |
| Nearest miss: online picker, but post-booking | KRUU | "Your photo booth is now reserved for the desired date. **In the customer portal**, you can choose your desired design online… and personalize it with text" (kruu.com/us/photo-booth-rental-los-angeles) — portal comes **after** reservation; text personalization, no live personalized print preview pre-booking |
| (d) True live pre-booking customizer | **none found** | Two targeted falsification sweeps ("design your template online / template editor / live preview LA", "'see your' / 'preview your' print names date 'before you book'") surfaced no LA/OC company offering it |

**Copy guidance.** The page may say, confidently and specifically:
- "Around here, the print is designed *after* you book — a proof arrives by
  email five days, sometimes thirty, before your event." (receipts above)
- "We couldn't find another Los Angeles or Orange County booth that shows you
  your own names on the print before you've paid anyone anything." (framed as
  surveyed fact, not omniscience)
- Never name competitors on the page; the receipts stay here.

**Verification date matters:** re-verify before any paid campaign leans on the
absolute claim.
